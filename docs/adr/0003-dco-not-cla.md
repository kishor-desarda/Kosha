# 3. Developer Certificate of Origin, not a contributor licence agreement

Status: accepted

**Situation.** The project needs assurance that contributions may be distributed under its licences.

**Options.** A contributor licence agreement (CLA) assigning broad rights to a steward, or the
Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) with a `Signed-off-by` line per commit.

**Decision.** DCO. Contributions stay under the licence of the folder they change.

**Consequences.** Contributing is simple, and no single party can relicense contributors' work.
CI checks that every commit in a pull request is signed off.
