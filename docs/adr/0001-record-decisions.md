# 1. Record architecture decisions

Status: accepted

**Situation.** Kosha is built by a changing group of people. Reasons behind choices get lost.

**Decision.** Every significant choice (a new dependency, a new interface, a licence question, a
change of direction) gets one short page here: situation, options, decision, consequences.

**Consequences.** Newcomers can see why things are as they are; decisions can be revisited openly.
