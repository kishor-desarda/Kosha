# Trademark policy

The Kosha code is open source: you may use, change and share it under its licences. The **name
"Kosha"** and the **Kosha logo** identify the official project, so they follow a few simple rules.
This policy is about names and logos only; it never limits what the licences allow you to do with
the code.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product, service or add-in **works with Kosha**, **is built for Kosha** or
  **supports Kosha**.
- Offer installation, hosting, training or support for Kosha, and say so.
- Use the name and logo in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews about Kosha.
- Distribute unmodified official releases under the name Kosha.

## Please do not

- Call a modified version "Kosha" or a confusingly similar name; give it its own name and say it
  is *based on Kosha*.
- Use the logo as part of your own product's logo, or suggest the project endorses you when it
  does not.
- Register domain names, company names or social accounts that suggest you are the official
  project.

If you are unsure, ask in a GitHub discussion. Changes to this policy are agreed by the
maintainers as described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
